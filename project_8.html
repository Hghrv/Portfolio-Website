Empirically proven methods for hyper-parameters optimisation and initialisation

Keras, Tensorflow, WatsonX prompt models

 

Visualising Neural Networks with JavaScript librairies and Node.js:

Unsupervised neural networks and hyper-parameter tunig

 

The idea is to test the projection of an neural network on a screen display in a dynamically animated network with high-speed transmission, with the aim of being able to tune the model for smoother transmission.

The amount of arrays or dots are the key weight-parameters to consider in operational research.

Combining CSS and Javascript highly improves display loading time

 

 

The display can be tested tested at a high speed with on-scroll animation requiring IntersectionObserver modules. A simple click on the visual restarts the networks with a central 'red' constraint. The whole model is set into rotation as the model would ideally be further visualised over a virtual earthglobe.

This is made possible with Javascript by adding our script in our project_8.html file. The dots are generated as a Network Optimisation model while the red dot can be centralised as a constraint to ensure a consistent forward and backpropagation of the model.

It is an effective way to assess the display and optimsise loading times for live prompting labs such as IBM WatsonX or Tensorflow.
